Clinical Registrant #3

MLH Explorations / Ethos Cannabis

Philadelphia-area CR with multiple dispensaries, partnered with Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University and its Lambert Center for cannabis research.

Overview

MLH Explorations operates under the Ethos Cannabis brand, serving the Philadelphia suburbs with multiple dispensary locations. As Clinical Registrant #3, Ethos brings a patient-focused approach to cannabis medicine, emphasizing real-world outcome data and clinical integration studies.

ACRC Partnership

Sidney Kimmel Medical College — Thomas Jefferson University, Philadelphia

Ethos partners with Jefferson's Lambert Center for the Study of Medicinal Cannabis and Hemp, one of the first academic centers in the nation dedicated specifically to cannabis research. The Lambert Center focuses on bridging the gap between patient-reported outcomes and clinical practice, with a strong emphasis on product consistency and evidence-based treatment recommendations.

Research Focus

  • Patient-reported outcomes: Collecting and analyzing real-world data from dispensary patients on symptom relief and quality of life
  • Product consistency: Studying the relationship between product formulation, cannabinoid profiles, and therapeutic effects
  • Clinical integration: Developing models for integrating cannabis medicine into conventional clinical practice
  • Education: Provider and patient education on evidence-based cannabis use through Jefferson's academic programs

Partnership Potential

The Ethos-Jefferson partnership is well-positioned for collaborations involving patient outcome data collection, dispensary-based research recruitment, and clinical education programs. The Lambert Center's focus on real-world evidence makes this an attractive partnership for organizations interested in pragmatic clinical research.
