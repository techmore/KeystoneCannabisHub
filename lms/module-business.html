Cannabis Business & Entrepreneurship

Learn the fundamentals of building a cannabis business in Pennsylvania. Covers licensing pathways, business plan development, capital requirements, operating models, and regulatory compliance for entrepreneurs.

● Business Track ⚙ Entrepreneurs & Executives ⏱ ~3 hours

What You'll Learn

  • PA cannabis license types: grower/processor, dispensary, clinical registrant
  • Business plan structure for cannabis ventures
  • Capital requirements and funding sources (equity, debt, SBIR, grants)
  • Operating models from our 10-model framework
  • Social equity programs and application scoring

📚 For Learners

Want to start a cannabis business? This module covers the real mechanics: licensing, capital, operating models, and social equity. Essential reading before you write a business plan or apply for a permit. Combine with Regulatory Affairs for a complete entrepreneurship track. Earns the Advanced Practitioner badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Entrepreneurship or cannabis business programs. Prerequisites: Compliance module. Learning objectives: 1) Compare PA cannabis license types, 2) Build a cannabis business plan, 3) Evaluate capital raising strategies. Assignment: Draft a one-page business concept for a PA cannabis venture, including license pathway, capital estimate, and operating model choice from the 10-model framework.

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 7.2 Competence, 10.3 Continual Improvement. Use this module to build business and strategic competence among your leadership team. Ensures managers understand the regulatory and financial realities of the PA market when making operational decisions.
