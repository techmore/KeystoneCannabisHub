The Opportunity

A vertically integrated education + production + social impact model.

Pennsylvania's cannabis landscape has a unique gap: there is no centralized, openly-available resource that connects the medical marijuana research infrastructure (Clinical Registrants + ACRC university partners) with the hands-on agricultural education happening at institutions like Delaware Valley University, the legal pathways for entry (hemp vs. medical), and the social equity goals of the commonwealth.

This hub aims to fill that gap — becoming the definitive reference point for anyone researching, investing in, studying, or advocating for cannabis in Pennsylvania. All information is sourced from public records, academic publications, and official state resources.
