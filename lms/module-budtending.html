LMS Module · Budtending

Budtending & Patient Consultation

Develop the knowledge and communication skills to guide patients to the right products. Covers cannabinoid science, consumption methods, dosing, terpenes, and consultation best practices.

● Dispensary Track ⚙ Patient-Facing Roles ⏱ ~2 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Cannabinoid profiles: THC, CBD, CBG, CBN, THCV and their effects
  • Terpene identification and therapeutic properties
  • Consumption method comparisons: inhalation, oral, topical, sublingual
  • Dosing principles: start low, go slow, titration strategies
  • Patient consultation script and intake best practices
