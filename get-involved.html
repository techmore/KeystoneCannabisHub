Get Involved

This hub is meant to grow. Whether you have information to contribute, want to partner, or are simply exploring — there are many ways to be part of building Pennsylvania's cannabis research and education ecosystem.

How You Can Contribute

Information

Submit Data or Corrections

If you have updated information about Clinical Registrants, dispensary locations, permit status, or regulatory changes — we want to include it. This site is built on public records and your contributions make it more accurate.

Partnership

Partner With the Project

If you represent a CR, university, dispensary, or nonprofit interested in education, research, or social impact — let's explore what collaboration could look like.

Education

Student or Educator Interest

Students and faculty interested in the work-study model, greenhouse training, or breeding research — your input on curriculum design and program structure is welcome.

Social Impact

Disability & Workforce Partners

If you work with OVR, Employment First, disability advocacy, or workforce re-entry programs — your expertise is critical to building an inclusive employment model.

Legal

Legal & Compliance Expertise

Attorneys, compliance officers, or regulators familiar with PA cannabis law — your guidance on zoning, permitting, and structuring is invaluable.

Science

Scientific Collaboration

Researchers in plant genomics, breeding, pathology, or controlled environment agriculture — let's discuss shared projects, data sharing, or joint grant applications.

Current Priorities

Here's what we're actively working on and where help is most needed:

  • Confirming current CR list: Exact 2026 roster of all 10 Clinical Registrants and their operating status
  • Dispensary location data: Mapping all CR dispensary locations with contact info and product focus
  • Del Val partnership development: Drafting outreach to Dr. Carla Garzon and the Plant Science department
  • Herbal Healers investigation: Verifying certifications, compliance history, and partnership interest
  • Zoning research: Determining specific permissibility of greenhouse/agricultural operations in Newtown/Doylestown area townships
  • Grant identification: Cataloging applicable state and federal grants for hemp research, workforce development, and disability employment

The Bigger Picture

The ultimate goal of this hub is to become Pennsylvania's most comprehensive, openly-available information resource for cannabis research, regulation, and education. Over time, we envision:

  • A searchable database of all PA cannabis research publications and ongoing studies
  • Interactive maps of CR dispensaries, grower/processors, and testing labs
  • A regulatory tracker with updates on DOH and PDA rule changes
  • A directory of educational programs and training opportunities
  • A job board connecting trained graduates with industry employers
  • An annual report on the state of PA cannabis research and social equity
