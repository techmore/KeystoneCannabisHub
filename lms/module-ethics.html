Ethics & Professionalism

Develop the professional standards and ethical frameworks needed to serve patients, colleagues, and the community with integrity in Pennsylvania's cannabis industry.

● Core Requirement ⚙ All Roles ⏱ ~1.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Professional codes of conduct for cannabis workers
  • Patient privacy, dignity, and informed consent
  • Diversity, equity, and inclusion in the cannabis workplace
  • Conflict resolution and professional communication
  • Reporting misconduct and whistleblower protections
