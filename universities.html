1. Thomas Jefferson University — MS in Medical Cannabis Science & Business

Location: Philadelphia, PA (100% Online)

Program Type: Master of Science (33 credits), plus 3 stackable graduate certificates

Established: Approximately 2020

Format: Fully online, asynchronous. Enroll fall, spring, or summer.

Program Overview

Jefferson offers the most comprehensive cannabis academic program in Pennsylvania — a full MS degree built from three stackable graduate certificates. It is delivered through the Institute of Emerging Health Professions and supported by the Lambert Center for the Study of Medicinal Cannabis & Hemp, which Jefferson established as the first comprehensive cannabis academic resource at a major health sciences university in the US.

Three Stackable Certificates

  • Cannabis Medicine (CMD) — Clinical applications, physiological impacts, therapies, and health effects. For clinicians and healthcare professionals.
  • Cannabis Science (CSC) — Botany, chemistry, pharmacology, toxicology, and forensic analysis of cannabis.
  • Cannabis Business (CBU) — Regulations, management, operations, financial analysis, and business model innovation.

Course List (11 courses, 33 credits)

  • CMD 505, 506, 507 — Cannabis Medicine courses
  • CSC 511, 512, 513 — Cannabis Science courses
  • CBU 501 — Emerging Issues in the Business of Cannabis
  • CBU 506 — Cannabis Financial & Operations Analysis
  • CBU 509 — Industrial Hemp, Process, and Products
  • CSO 522 — Cannabis Policy, Politics & Regulation
  • MBX 604 — Business Model Innovation
  • CRS 600, CRS 610 — Research courses

Key Faculty

Ruth Charbonneau, JD, RN

Role: Program Director, Graduate Cannabis Program; Assistant Professor; Associate Director, Lambert Center

Background: JD and RN, combining legal expertise with clinical healthcare experience. Leads the MS program and Lambert Center operations. Teaches Emerging Issues in the Cannabis Industry.

Brooke Worster, MD

Role: Medical Advisor, Graduate Cannabis Program; Associate Professor; Enterprise Director, Supportive Oncology

Background: Physician specializing in supportive oncology and palliative care at Jefferson. Developed the MS program to advance evidence-based education around emerging therapies. Oversees clinical curriculum.

Program Longevity & Recognition

Jefferson was the first major health sciences university in the US to offer a comprehensive cannabis academic resource. The Lambert Center was established to sponsor scientific research and engagement focused on cannabis and hemp. The MS program has been running for several cohorts and is well-established.

2. Wilkes University — Cannabis Chemistry (BS) & Certificates

Location: Wilkes-Barre, PA

Programs: BS in Cannabis Chemistry (120 credits), Cannabis Production & CBD Extraction Certificate (16 credits, hybrid), Medicinal Cannabis for Health Care Professionals Certificate (16 credits)

Established: Certificate ~2021, BS major ~2022

BS in Cannabis Chemistry

Full 120-credit bachelor's degree meeting American Chemical Society (ACS) certification requirements. On-campus, with access to growth chambers and greenhouses. Covers growing techniques, compound extraction, quality control, and chemical analysis. Graduates qualify for ACS certification.

Cannabis Production & CBD Extraction Certificate (16 credits, hybrid)

Designed for pharmacy, biology, and chemistry students as well as industry professionals. Two-year program covering:

  • PHA 461 — Cannabis Law and Policy (1 credit)
  • PHA 462 — Hemp Biology and Physiology with Lab (3+1 credits)
  • PHA 463 — Cannabis and Cannabinoids (3 credits)
  • PHA 464 — CBD Analysis and Extraction Lab (2 credits)
  • PHA 465 — Therapeutics of Medicinal Cannabis (3 credits)
  • PHA 466 — Capstone Research (3 credits)

Medicinal Cannabis for Health Care Professionals (16 credits)

Graduate-level certificate covering cannabis law, therapeutics (3-course sequence), and cannabinoid science. Includes a capstone research project.

Key Faculty

Doctoral-level faculty from pharmacy, chemistry, and biology teach all courses. The cross-disciplinary nature provides broad perspective on analytical methods and therapeutic applications.

3. York College of Pennsylvania — BS in Cannabis Chemistry

Location: York, PA

Program: BS in Cannabis Chemistry (on-campus)

Established: 2023 — first program of its kind on the East Coast

Program Overview

A specialized bachelor's degree combining horticulture, toxicology, chemical analysis, biochemistry, and pharmacology. Students take courses in molecular biology, biochemistry, natural products chemistry, instrumental analysis, and cannabis-specific coursework.

Sample Courses

  • BIO 150/151 — Introduction to Molecular Biology + Lab
  • BIO 350/351 — Biochemistry I + Lab
  • CHM 160 — Natural Products and Society (1 credit cannabis intro)
  • Quantitative Analysis (cannabis-specific section)
  • Instrumental Analysis (cannabis-specific section)

Key Faculty

James B. Foresman, PhD

Role: Professor of Chemistry

Background: Teaches general chemistry, physical chemistry, computational chemistry, and cannabis chemistry. Research interests include modeling protein-ligand interactions, computational solvent effects, and Python-based tools for biomolecular study. Key architect of the cannabis chemistry program.

Additional faculty include Dr. Foy, who teaches the quantitative and instrumental analysis courses specific to the cannabis program.

4. Pennsylvania Institute of Technology — Cannabis Studies

Location: Media, PA (online & hybrid options)

Programs: BS in Cannabis Business (123 credits), Associate in Cannabis Studies (Business, Health Therapy, Horticulture)

Established: 2019 — first regionally accredited college in the US to offer undergraduate cannabis degrees

Program Overview

P.I.T. was the first regionally accredited college in the nation to offer undergraduate degrees in cannabis, launching in July 2019. They offer three distinct associate tracks:

  • Cannabis Business — Entrepreneurship, dispensary management, supply chain, marketing, regulatory compliance
  • Cannabis Health Therapy — Medical applications, patient care, endocannabinoid system, therapeutic protocols
  • Cannabis Horticulture — Cultivation, propagation, breeding, controlled environment production, post-harvest

A BS in Cannabis Business (123 credits) launched more recently for students seeking a four-year degree.

Key Faculty

Lou Giannotti, PharmD

Role: Director of Cannabis Studies

Background: Pharmacist with 20+ years as a Pharmacy District Supervisor for community pharmacies. Former Director of College Relations and Professional Recruitment for Rite Aid. Former Director of Dispensary Operations for Holistic Industries / Liberty Cannabis in PA. Joined P.I.T. in 2019 to launch and lead the cannabis programs. Teaches many courses in the curriculum and identifies industry partnership opportunities.

Program Longevity

P.I.T. has the longest continuously running cannabis degree program in Pennsylvania, now in its 7th year. Hundreds of graduates have entered the workforce in dispensaries, grow operations, and cannabis businesses across the state.

6. Delaware Valley University — Horticulture with Cannabis Specialization

Location: Doylestown, PA

Programs: BS in Horticulture with Cannabis Specialization, Cannabis Certificate (17 credits), Green Flower non-credit certificates

Established: Certificate ~2021, program active in research since 2019

Program Overview

Del Val offers a science-based cannabis curriculum grounded in horticulture and plant science. The program uses industrial hemp (≤0.3% THC) for all hands-on activities. Students work in greenhouses, labs, and field trials. The program emphasizes experiential learning and practical skills.

Courses

  • PS 3002 — Cannabis I (cannabis biology, history, pharmacology, legal issues, business basics)
  • PS 3006 — Cannabis II (applied hemp production, processing, business development)
  • PS 4209 — Greenhouse Management
  • PS 3231 — Hydroponics
  • LAES 3208 — Medicinal Plant Practicum
  • PS 4106 — Marketing Horticultural Products

Key Faculty

Dr. Carla Garzon

Role: K.H. Littlefield Endowed Professor of Plant Science; Leader, Cannabis Research Program

Background: PhD from Penn State. Former faculty at Oklahoma State University. Published 40+ peer-reviewed papers in plant pathology and horticulture. Serves on the board of Pennsylvania's Center for Plant Excellence. Research focuses on hemp production in controlled environments, plant pathology, disease management, and crop breeding for resilient varieties.

Research collaborators: USDA-ARS, Cornell University, Penn State, University of Wisconsin, Oklahoma State University.

Green Flower Non-Credit Certificates

Del Val also partners with Green Flower to offer three 9-week online certificates: Cultivation Specialist, Retail Specialist, and Extraction & Product Development (each $799).

10. Alvernia University — Cannabis Academy

Location: Reading, PA (online)

Program: Alvernia Cannabis Academy (non-credit certificates via Cleveland School of Cannabis)

Established: April 2025

Alvernia partners with the Cleveland School of Cannabis (CSC) — the only cannabis school accredited by a US Department of Education-recognized accreditor — to offer self-paced online certificate programs. 12. Penn State College of Medicine — CCNPP (Research Center)

Location: Hershey, PA

Entity: Center for Cannabis and Natural Product Pharmaceutics (CCNPP)

Established: 2018 (ACRC designation)

Overview

Penn State College of Medicine was one of the first eight universities approved as an Academic Clinical Research Center (ACRC) in Pennsylvania's Medical Marijuana Program. The CCNPP was established to expand upon the scientific progress of the ACRC. While not a degree-granting program, it is Pennsylvania's leading cannabis research center and collaborates with multiple institutions.

Research Focus

  • Pain management (including chemotherapeutic-induced peripheral neuropathy)
  • Anti-cancer activity of cannabinoids
  • Inflammatory bowel disease
  • Endometriosis pain (CBD clinical trial ongoing)
  • Neurobehavioral effects of alcohol and cannabis co-use

CR Partner

PA Options for Wellness (10-year research agreement signed June 2019).

Collaborators

Rutgers University, Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine, Drexel University College of Medicine, Geisinger Commonwealth College of Medicine.

Note: The following schools also serve as ACRC partners in PA's Medical Marijuana Program but do not offer standalone cannabis degree/certificate programs: Temple University (Lewis Katz School of Medicine, partnered with Cresco), University of Pennsylvania (Perelman School of Medicine, partnered with Curaleaf), Drexel University (College of Medicine, partnered with Agronomed Biologics), Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine (partnered with Organic Remedies), Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine (partnered with Ayr Wellness), University of Pittsburgh (School of Medicine, partnered with Goodblend), Duquesne University (College of Osteopathic Medicine, partnered with Terra Farm), and Geisinger Commonwealth. See the Clinical Registrants page for details on their research partnerships.
