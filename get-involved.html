Get Involved

This hub and venture is being built in the open. Whether you have information to share, want to partner, or are simply exploring — there are concrete ways to contribute right now.

How You Can Contribute

Each of these is a real need. Pick the one that fits you.

Information

Submit Data or Corrections

What's needed: Updated CR rosters, dispensary addresses, permit status changes, zoning codes, university program updates, and any corrections to what we have.

Impact: Makes this the most accurate public resource for PA cannabis research.

Partnership

Partner With the Venture

Who we need: CRs interested in a satellite grow/research site, universities wanting greenhouse access for students, disability advocacy organizations, workforce development programs, grant partners, and investors aligned with the social mission.

Impact: Directly builds the operational model.

Education

Design the Work-Study Curriculum

What's needed: Educators and students to co-design the greenhouse work-study program structure — learning objectives, rotation schedules, credit arrangements, and skill assessments. We have the concept; we need the curriculum.

Impact: Shapes what students will learn and how they'll be prepared for industry careers.

Social Impact

Connect With Disability & Workforce Orgs

Who we need: Staff or clients of OVR, Employment First programs, InVEST, disability advocacy nonprofits, and workforce re-entry organizations. Your real-world experience is essential to building an employment model that actually works.

Impact: Ensures the social mission is grounded in reality, not theory.

Legal

Legal & Zoning Research

What's needed: Pro bono or low-cost legal research on: Bucks County township zoning for greenhouse/agricultural uses, PA hemp permit application assistance, business entity formation (LLC / B-Corp / hybrid), and IP agreements for university partnerships.

Impact: Keeps everything compliant and properly structured from day one.

Science

Scientific Collaboration

What's needed: Researchers in plant genomics, cannabis breeding, plant pathology, or controlled environment ag who want to collaborate on grant applications, share data, co-author papers, or advise on the MinION workflow and change-log breeding methodology.

Impact: Brings scientific rigor to the breeding and production program.

Ways to Help Right Now (No Commitment)

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be in Pennsylvania to help?

No. Information contributions, research, and advocacy can happen from anywhere. Physical presence matters for the greenhouse operation itself and for zoning/legal research, but the information hub is global.

Is this a paid opportunity?

At this stage, contributions are voluntary. We're working toward grant funding that could support paid positions for curriculum design, research, and legal work. If you're willing to volunteer now, you'll be first in line when funding arrives.

I work for a CR / Del Val / a dispensary — can I get in trouble for contributing?

We only use publicly available information. If you're sharing something not public, you should check your employer's policy first. We welcome official submissions through proper channels.

Can I invest or donate money?

Not yet. The legal entity hasn't been formed. When it is, we'll update this page with options for impact investment, grants, or donations depending on the structure chosen.

I have cannabis industry experience — how can I help most?

Review our plans and tell us what's unrealistic, what's missing, or what we're underestimating. Real-world feedback from people who've done this is the most valuable contribution you can make.
