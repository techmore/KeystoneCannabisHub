Clinical Registrant #8

Story of PA / Goodblend

Western Pennsylvania CR serving the Pittsburgh region, partnered with University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and Geisinger Commonwealth for population health research.

Overview

Story of PA, operating under the Goodblend brand, holds Clinical Registrant #8 status and serves the Western Pennsylvania and Pittsburgh region. Goodblend's unique dual-ACRC partnership — combining the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine with Geisinger Commonwealth — positions this CR for large-scale population health research and health data integration studies.

ACRC Partnership

University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine & Geisinger Commonwealth

This dual-partnership model is distinctive among the CRs. The University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine is a leading research institution with strengths in biomedical research and clinical medicine. Geisinger Commonwealth brings access to Geisinger's extensive health system data, electronic health records, and population health research infrastructure. Together, they provide a powerful platform for studying cannabis outcomes at the population level.

Dispensary Locations

Status: Operations Ceased (2023)

Goodblend Pennsylvania LLC announced the closure of its Pennsylvania operations in July 2023, shuttering its Pittsburgh cultivation facility and laying off 76 employees. Its two dispensaries — in Pittsburgh's Friendship neighborhood and Erie — also closed. This serves as a case study in the risks facing CRs in PA's capped market.

Goodblend operated two dispensaries in Western Pennsylvania under the goodblend brand (a retail brand of Parallel). All locations are now closed.

Research Focus

  • Population health studies: Large-scale research on cannabis use patterns and health outcomes across patient populations
  • Health data integration: Leveraging Geisinger's electronic health records for longitudinal outcomes research
  • Clinical research: Pitt's biomedical research capabilities support clinical studies on cannabis therapeutics
  • Public health: Studying the public health implications of medical cannabis access in Pennsylvania

Partnership Potential

Goodblend's dual-ACRC structure and Geisinger's health data systems create unique opportunities for population health research. Potential collaborations include health data analysis partnerships, epidemiologic studies, clinical trial recruitment through the Geisinger network, and student research placements at both Pitt and Geisinger.
