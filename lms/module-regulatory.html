Regulatory Affairs & Policy

Advanced module on cannabis regulatory strategy, policy analysis, government relations, and compliance program design. For professionals navigating Pennsylvania's evolving cannabis regulations.

● Advanced Track ⚙ Compliance & Policy Pros ⏱ ~2.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • PA regulatory framework and legislative process
  • Building and managing a compliance program
  • DOH audit preparation and response
  • Policy research and advocacy strategies
  • Federal vs. state regulatory conflicts and 280E implications

📚 For Learners

Regulatory affairs is one of the highest-paid career tracks in cannabis — and the most critical. This module prepares you for compliance officer, policy analyst, and government relations roles. Combine with the Compliance module for a complete regulatory career foundation. Earns the Compliance Pro badge (at Level 4+).

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Capstone or policy-focused courses. Prerequisites: Compliance module. Learning objectives: 1) Design a compliance program, 2) Prepare for a DOH audit, 3) Analyze proposed cannabis legislation. Assignment: Analyze a proposed PA cannabis bill and write a one-page impact assessment covering operational, financial, and compliance implications.

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 10.2 Nonconformity and Corrective Action, 7.2 Competence, 9.1 Monitoring. This module builds advanced regulatory competence within your compliance team. Ensures your organization can design, implement, and audit a compliance program that meets DOH expectations and ISO 9001 standards. Essential for leadership roles.
