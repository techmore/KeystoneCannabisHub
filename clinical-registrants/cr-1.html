Clinical Registrant #1

PA Options for Wellness

Central PA's first Clinical Registrant, partnered with Penn State College of Medicine for broad medical cannabis research and patient outcomes.

Overview

PA Options for Wellness was awarded Clinical Registrant #1 status and holds both a grower/processor permit and dispensary permits in the Central PA region. As the first CR designated by the Pennsylvania Department of Health, the organization set the template for the CR-ACRC research partnership model that defines the program today.

ACRC Partnership

Penn State College of Medicine — Hershey

A 10-year research agreement was signed in June 2019 between PA Options for Wellness and Penn State's College of Medicine. The partnership operates through the Center for Cannabis and Natural Product Pharmacology (CCNPP), which conducts clinical studies on the efficacy and safety of medical cannabis. Penn State brings one of Pennsylvania's leading medical research infrastructures to the collaboration, with a focus on translating patient outcomes into published clinical research.

Dispensary Locations

PA Options for Wellness operates six retail locations under the Vytal Options brand. All are medical-only dispensaries serving registered PA patients.

Research Focus

  • Pain management: Clinical studies on medical cannabis efficacy for chronic pain conditions
  • Anti-cancer activity: Investigating cannabinoid effects on cancer cell lines and symptom management
  • Health outcomes: Longitudinal patient outcome tracking and quality-of-life assessments
  • Product safety: Consistent dosing, formulation testing, and contaminant screening

Partnership Potential

PA Options for Wellness and Penn State's CCNPP represent a well-established research pipeline. Potential collaboration opportunities include patient outcome studies, work-study placements for students in clinical research settings, and satellite grow or processing collaborations that feed into the existing Penn State research protocols.
