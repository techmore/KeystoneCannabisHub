Clinical Registrant #7

Curaleaf PA

Greater Philadelphia CR operating a statewide network, partnered with the Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania for high-caliber clinical research.

Overview

Curaleaf PA, part of the largest multi-state cannabis operator in the United States, holds Clinical Registrant #7 status. With a statewide dispensary network and significant operational scale, Curaleaf brings substantial resources to its research partnership with UPenn's Perelman School of Medicine — one of the top medical research institutions in the world.

ACRC Partnership

Perelman School of Medicine — University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia

The Perelman School of Medicine is consistently ranked among the top medical schools in the nation and brings world-class research infrastructure to the partnership. UPenn's expertise spans neurology, oncology, pharmacology, and clinical trial design. The collaboration focuses on rigorous clinical studies leveraging UPenn's extensive academic medical center resources.

Locations

Curaleaf operates a broad network of dispensaries under multiple brand names across Pennsylvania, with a strong presence in the Greater Philadelphia region and expanding statewide coverage. The dispensary network provides one of the broadest patient access footprints among the CRs.

Research Focus

  • Epilepsy studies: Clinical research on cannabinoid efficacy for seizure disorders and epilepsy management
  • Palliative cancer treatment: Outpatient studies on cannabis for symptom management in cancer patients
  • Pain management: Investigating cannabis-based treatments for various pain conditions
  • Clinical trial infrastructure: Leveraging UPenn's clinical research capabilities for high-quality study design and execution

Partnership Potential

The Curaleaf-UPenn partnership represents the highest-caliber research infrastructure among the CRs. UPenn's Perelman School of Medicine offers unparalleled clinical research capabilities, and Curaleaf's scale provides broad patient access for recruitment. Potential collaborations include clinical trial partnerships, fellowship programs, and translational research initiatives connecting basic science to patient outcomes.
