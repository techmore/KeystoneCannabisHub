Clinical Registrant #9

Terra Farm Inc.

Western Pennsylvania CR partnered with Duquesne University College of Osteopathic Medicine for osteopathic research and cultivation-to-patient outcomes.

Overview

Terra Farm Inc. holds Clinical Registrant #9 status and serves the Western Pennsylvania region. As a smaller-scale CR compared to the multi-state operators in the program, Terra Farm's partnership with Duquesne University reflects a focused, research-oriented approach to cannabis medicine with an emphasis on the connection between cultivation practices and patient outcomes.

ACRC Partnership

Duquesne University College of Osteopathic Medicine — Pittsburgh

Duquesne University's College of Osteopathic Medicine is a newer osteopathic medical school with a strong commitment to community health and whole-patient care. The partnership with Terra Farm focuses on the full cultivation-to-patient outcome cycle, examining how growing practices and product characteristics affect therapeutic results. Duquesne brings a research perspective rooted in osteopathic principles and Pittsburgh-area community health.

Dispensary Locations

Terra Farm operates dispensaries under the TerraVida Holistic Centers brand in southeastern Pennsylvania. All are medical-only.

Research Focus

  • Osteopathic research: Cannabis therapeutics through the lens of osteopathic whole-patient medicine
  • Cultivation-to-patient outcomes: Tracking how growing methods, genetics, and processing affect patient therapeutic responses
  • Product consistency: Research on standardized cultivation protocols for reliable patient outcomes
  • Community health: Studying cannabis access and outcomes in Western Pennsylvania communities

Partnership Potential

Terra Farm's collaboration with Duquesne offers opportunities for research on the relationship between cultivation practices and clinical outcomes — a relatively underexplored area in cannabis science. Potential collaborations include cultivation protocol studies, student research programs in plant science and medicine, and community health research in Western Pennsylvania.
