Clinical Registrant #6

Organic Remedies

South-central Pennsylvania CR with multiple dispensaries, partnered with Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine for a multi-study research program.

Overview

Organic Remedies holds Clinical Registrant #6 status and operates a network of dispensaries across south-central Pennsylvania. The organization emphasizes a holistic approach to cannabis medicine and has developed one of the most comprehensive multi-study research programs among the CRs, covering behavior, quality of life, cognition, chronic pain, and opioid management.

ACRC Partnership

Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine (PCOM)

Organic Remedies partners with PCOM, a leading osteopathic medical school with a strong tradition of whole-patient care and community health research. PCOM's research programs focus on clinical outcomes, patient behavior, and the integration of cannabis medicine into osteopathic practice. The partnership supports multiple concurrent studies, making it one of the more active CR-ACRC research collaborations in the state.

Locations

Organic Remedies operates dispensaries throughout south-central Pennsylvania, serving communities in the Harrisburg, York, and Lancaster regions. The dispensary network provides consistent access to patients across a broad geographic area.

Research Focus

  • Behavioral research: Studying how medical cannabis affects patient behavior, mood, and daily functioning
  • Quality of life: Longitudinal assessments of patient-reported quality of life outcomes
  • Cognition: Investigating cannabis effects on cognitive function in medical patients
  • Chronic pain: Multi-study program examining cannabis efficacy for various chronic pain conditions
  • Opioid management: Researching cannabis as an adjunct or alternative to opioid pain management

Partnership Potential

Organic Remedies' multi-study research program creates multiple entry points for collaboration. The PCOM partnership provides a strong osteopathic medical framework, and the breadth of research areas offers opportunities for student research placements, clinical trial support, and community health studies in south-central Pennsylvania.
