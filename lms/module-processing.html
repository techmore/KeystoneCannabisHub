Cannabis Extraction & Processing

Learn the science and craft of cannabis extraction and post-harvest processing. Covers BHO extraction, ethanol extraction, CO2 extraction, rosin pressing, distillation, and product formulation.

● Processing Track ⚙ Extractors & Lab Techs ⏱ ~3 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Solvent-based vs. solventless extraction methods
  • Hydrocarbon (BHO/PHO) extraction principles and safety
  • CO2 extraction equipment and process parameters
  • Ethanol extraction for broad-spectrum products
  • Distillation, winterization, decarboxylation, and formulation

📚 For Learners

Extraction technicians are among the highest-paid entry-level roles in cannabis. This module gives you the theory behind every concentrate on the shelf. Pair with Analytics for a complete lab skill set. Earns the Processing Specialist badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Alongside organic chemistry or separation science courses. Prerequisites: Basic chemistry recommended. Learning objectives: 1) Compare solvent vs. solventless extraction methods, 2) Explain distillation principles, 3) Identify safety requirements for hydrocarbon extraction. Discussion prompt: How does extraction method selection affect final product profile and patient application?

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 8.5.1 Control of Production, 10.2 Nonconformity. Use this module to establish baseline extraction knowledge across your processing team. Combined with facility-specific SOPs, it creates an auditable training record for GMP compliance. Workers at Level 3+ can operate independently on standard extraction runs.
