V1 Concept — Free for Workers

PA Cannabis Talent Network

A free industry job board with skill profiling — think Big 5 personality but for professional capabilities. Build a multidimensional skill profile, get matched blind to employers across the entire PA cannabis industry, and stay subscribed through ongoing module updates and curated legal intelligence.

The Core Idea: Skill Profiles, Not Pass/Fail

Most assessments tell you whether you passed or failed. This isn't that. Instead, you build a multidimensional skill profile across dozens of dimensions — patient consultation, inventory accuracy, compliance knowledge, leadership, systems proficiency — each on a spectrum from emerging to expert. Like a Big 5 personality profile, but for what you can do. Employers see the profile blind and decide if the shape fits their needs. No pass, no fail — just a clear picture of where you are and where you could grow.

This is the V1 concept — a first public version to establish the offering. It lives alongside the LMS Portal but is independent: you don't need to complete LMS modules to build a profile, though doing both gives you the strongest signal to employers.

The Problem

Right now, getting hired in PA cannabis comes down to who you know, where you went to school, and whether your resume happens to match a job description. Good workers get passed over because they don't have the right brand name or connection. And there's no single place to find every cannabis job in the state — listings are scattered across LinkedIn, Indeed, individual company sites, and word of mouth.

  • Workers have no way to prove what they can actually do beyond a resume
  • Employers waste time interviewing mismatches — a skill profile would tell them in seconds
  • The industry has no centralized job board, so good candidates miss good openings
  • Bias — name, school, gender, and network filter out capable people before they're even seen

How It Works

1

Build Your Skill Profile (Free)

Answer scenario-based questions and self-assessments across skill dimensions relevant to your target roles. The result is a radar-style profile showing your relative strengths across areas like patient consultation, inventory management, compliance knowledge, systems proficiency, and more. No pass/fail — just a clear picture of your shape as a candidate. The profile is yours to update as you grow.

If you've also completed LMS Portal modules, those completion records feed into your profile automatically for a stronger signal.

2

Browse the Industry Job Board (Free)

Every cannabis job in PA in one place — dispensaries, grow facilities, processors, labs, hemp farms, consulting firms, and ancillary businesses. Not just CRs. Not just medical. The full industry. Listings are curated and verified to avoid scams. Workers browse, apply, and get discovered — all free.

3

Employers Define What They Need (Subscription)

Employers subscribe to post jobs, search blind profiles, and define success criteria for each role — not pass/fail requirements, but ideal profile shapes. The platform learns which profile patterns predict retention and performance for each specific employer, and refines matches over time.

4

Blind Matching

Employers see candidate profiles with all identifying information removed — no name, school, age, gender, or photo. Only skill dimensions, experience level, availability, and a compatibility score against the role's target profile. Shortlist based on fit alone. Unblinding happens only at interview. The result: candidates get considered for what they can actually do, not where they're from or who they know.

Role-Specific Tracks

Every track aligns with both the LMS training modules and the skills assessment. A candidate progresses through: LMS training → skills assessment → blind placement. Each track includes specific competencies tested, certifications earned, and the types of roles it maps to.

🏡 Front-End Sales & Patient Care

For dispensary agents, patient consultants, and retail leads. The public face of any cannabis business.

  • Patient consultation & needs assessment
  • Product knowledge across all categories (flower, vapes, concentrates, edibles, topicals, tinctures)
  • POS system operation & transaction compliance
  • Customer service scenarios & de-escalation
  • HIPAA compliance & patient privacy in practice
  • Recommendation-driven selling (not just order-taking)

📦 Back-End Fulfillment & Inventory

For inventory specialists, fulfillment leads, and operations coordinators. The engine room of every dispensary and processor.

  • Inventory receiving, verification & reconciliation
  • Seed-to-sale system data entry & compliance
  • Waste documentation & destruction protocols
  • Product staging, rotation & expiration management
  • Physical inventory counts & discrepancy investigation
  • Supply chain coordination with cultivation & processing

🌱 Cultivation & Production

For grow technicians, cultivation leads, and facility managers. The people who produce the product.

  • Plant life cycle management (clone to harvest)
  • Nutrient management & irrigation systems
  • Integrated pest management (IPM) protocols
  • Environmental monitoring & HVAC control
  • Harvest, trim, cure & packaging
  • Data collection for breeding & phenotype tracking

⚙ Processing & Extraction

For extraction technicians, processing leads, and lab assistants. The people who transform plant into product.

  • Extraction equipment operation (CO2, ethanol, hydrocarbon)
  • Solvent safety, handling & storage protocols
  • Post-processing: winterization, distillation, crystallization
  • Quality control sampling & in-process testing
  • GMP sanitation & contamination prevention
  • Batch tracking & lot reconciliation

📊 Accounting, Finance & Administration

For accountants, bookkeepers, and admin staff. The backbone that keeps cannabis businesses compliant with tax law and payroll.

  • IRC 280E implications for cannabis businesses
  • Inventory valuation & cost of goods sold (COGS) tracking
  • Cash management, deposit procedures & reconciliation
  • Payroll processing with cannabis-specific considerations
  • Sales tax & excise tax reporting
  • Compliance reporting for DOH & PDA audits

🖥 Technology, Systems & Data

For system administrators, data analysts, and tech support. The people who keep the digital infrastructure running.

  • METRC / seed-to-sale platform administration
  • POS system configuration & troubleshooting
  • Inventory management software operation
  • Data analysis & compliance reporting
  • Security system monitoring & access control
  • Integration between platforms (POS + METRC + accounting)

📈 Management & Leadership

For department heads, store managers, and aspiring leaders. The people who build and run teams.

  • Team management, scheduling & performance reviews
  • Regulatory compliance program management
  • Financial planning, P&L analysis & budgeting
  • Cannabis-specific HR, hiring & employee policies
  • Strategic planning & operations scaling
  • Social equity program design & inclusive hiring

🌎 Compliance & Regulatory

For compliance officers, auditors, and quality assurance staff. The people who keep the license.

  • DOH inspection preparation & response
  • Recordkeeping & documentation management
  • Standard operating procedure (SOP) writing & maintenance
  • Product recall & adverse event reporting protocols
  • Employee training program management
  • Third-party lab audit & COA verification

How the Matching Works

For workers (free):

  1. Create a profile and build your skill dimensions through scenario assessments
  2. Browse the industry job board — all PA cannabis listings in one place
  3. Set preferences: location, role type, schedule, company size
  4. Get notified when an employer's target profile matches your skill shape
  5. Employer sees your blind profile — if they shortlist, you're unblinded and can connect

No cost to workers, ever. The profile is portable — leave it active while employed, update it as you grow, and let the next opportunity find you.

For employers (subscription):

  1. Post unlimited jobs to the industry board
  2. Define target skill profiles for each role — not pass/fail requirements, but the ideal shape
  3. Search blind candidate profiles ranked by fit score
  4. Shortlist and unblind candidates when ready to interview
  5. After hire, provide outcome data — the platform learns what profile shapes actually perform at your operation
  6. Receive ongoing value: curated legal news updates, module release notifications, and industry benchmark reports

For universities (partnership):

  1. Integrate curriculum data so graduate profiles reflect actual coursework
  2. Receive anonymized reports on graduate skill trends and placement outcomes
  3. Identify gaps between curriculum and industry demand
  4. Refer graduates to the platform as a free career resource

Why People Stay Subscribed

The platform is free for workers. For employers, the subscription delivers ongoing value that justifies itself month after month:

Curated Legal & Regulatory Updates

Every week, the platform scrapes and summarizes DOH announcements, PDA rule changes, legislative bills, and court decisions relevant to PA cannabis. Employers get a 5-minute briefing instead of monitoring multiple government sites.

Module & Assessment Updates

When regulations change, the relevant LMS modules and skill assessment dimensions update automatically. Subscribers get notified, and their workforce training stays current without manual effort.

Industry Benchmark Reports

Quarterly anonymized reports on skill trends, salary ranges, hiring velocity, and role demand across PA cannabis. Employers use this data to set competitive offers and plan workforce development.

Unlimited Job Posting & Search

Post as many jobs as needed. Search the full candidate pool by skill profile shape. Get ranked matches without wading through unqualified applicants.

The Full Ecosystem

The LMS Portal provides the training backbone. The Talent Network provides skill profiling, the job board, and blind matching. Together they form a complete talent infrastructure — but each works independently. A worker can use the job board without LMS training, and an employer can post jobs without using the LMS modules.
