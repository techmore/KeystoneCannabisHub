Delaware Valley University Cannabis Program

Del Val (Doylestown, PA) runs one of the region's most active hemp/cannabis research and education programs, led by Dr. Carla Garzon. It is the natural academic partner for any greenhouse-based education, clone production, or breeding initiative in Bucks County.

Program Overview

Delaware Valley University's Cannabis Research Program is grounded in applied plant science with a focus on industrial hemp (low-THC cannabis, ≤0.3% Delta-9 THC). The program emphasizes hands-on, experiential learning — students work directly in greenhouses, labs, and field trials.

Dr. Carla Garzon — Program Lead

Title: K.H. Littlefield Endowed Professor of Plant Science

Background: PhD from Penn State. Former faculty at Oklahoma State University. Extensive publication record in plant pathology and horticulture (>40 peer-reviewed papers).

Research Focus:

  • Hemp production in controlled environments (greenhouses, indoor systems)
  • Plant pathology & disease management — emerging hemp diseases in PA, fungicide resistance, population genetics of pathogens
  • Crop breeding — developing resilient varieties (pest/disease resistance, northern climate adaptation). Includes germplasm work with USDA-ARS
  • Low-cannabinoid educational varieties for safe classroom use
  • Product development & processing standardization

Advocacy: She is a strong advocate for reducing stigma around hemp/cannabis and highlighting its broad industrial and educational potential. Her work prioritizes practical, research-backed solutions that directly help growers.

Dr. Garzon's Professional Network

  • Academic: Penn State (PhD alma mater), Cornell University, University of Wisconsin, Oklahoma State University, USDA-ARS
  • State/Industry: Board of Pennsylvania's Center for Plant Excellence, PA Hemp Summits, grower education initiatives
  • Professional: International Society for Plant Pathology, American Phytopathological Society, journal reviewer

Academic Programs

Horticulture Major with Cannabis Specialization (B.S.)

A full four-year degree with focused coursework on cannabis science, production, and business. Students get hands-on greenhouse and lab experience integrated into the curriculum.

Stand-Alone Cannabis Certificate (17 credits, ~1 year)

Available full-time or part-time. Covers the science of cannabis cultivation, processing, and regulatory compliance. Ideal for career-changers and working professionals.

Partnership Opportunities

The Del Val program is actively seeking industry and community partnerships. A greenhouse education + clone production + work-study model aligns directly with Dr. Garzon's priorities:

Hands-On Infrastructure

Co-develop additional greenhouse space, clone production facilities, or breeding resources to support experiential learning and student cohorts.

Student Pipeline

Create paid or credited internships and work-study roles. Dr. Garzon emphasizes real-world experience — a key gap she's working to fill.

Research Collaboration

Contribute to pathology, breeding, or product development projects. Co-author papers, apply for USDA and state grants together.

Vertical Integration Demo

Bridge production to processing/distribution (e.g., via Herbal Healers). Show real-world application of Del Val's protocols.

Funding & Grants

Co-apply for state/federal hemp research and workforce development grants. Your project could elevate Del Val's program profile.

Social Impact Layer

Integrate employment for people with disabilities — adds compelling equity/workforce development dimension to grant applications.

Why Del Val for This Project?

  • Existing hemp program means compliance framework is already established
  • Dr. Garzon is actively seeking to expand hands-on training capacity
  • Alumni already working in cultivation, research (including USDA hemp germplasm), and industry roles
  • Strong regional reputation and accreditation
