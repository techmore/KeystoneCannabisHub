Herbal Healers Wellness Dispensary

An existing compliant retail partner at 557 Durham Rd Unit A, Newtown, PA 18940 — a Farm Bill-compliant CBD/THCA wellness dispensary with third-party lab testing and an education-first approach.

Overview

Herbal Healers Wellness Dispensary is a retail operation in Newtown, PA, positioned as a wellness-oriented provider of hemp-derived cannabinoid products. Their business model emphasizes:

  • Compliance: All products are tested by third-party labs with Certificates of Analysis (COAs) available to customers
  • Farm Bill compliance: Products contain <0.3% Delta-9 THC by dry weight
  • Education: Staff are trained to help customers understand cannabinoid profiles, dosing, and product selection
  • Transparency: Lab results and sourcing information are openly shared

Why They Matter to This Project

Herbal Healers represents a critical piece of the vertical integration puzzle:

  • Existing retail channel: No need to build a dispensary from scratch — a compliant sales outlet already exists
  • Shared values: Education and transparency focus aligns with the Del Val / work-study mission
  • Location: 557 Durham Rd, Newtown, PA is minutes from Del Val's Doylestown campus
  • Product pipeline: A greenhouse + clone operation could supply Herbal Healers with flower, clones, and processed products directly
  • Certificate holder: Herbal Healers staff likely hold relevant certifications — the project can build on existing credentialing infrastructure

Vertical Integration Model

A simplified diagram of how the pieces fit together:

Del Val (education, research, student pipeline) → Greenhouse / Clone Operation (hands-on training, production, breeding) → Processing (extraction, formulation, packaging) → Herbal Healers (retail, distribution, customer education)

Each step in this chain creates learning opportunities for students and employment opportunities for target populations, while generating revenue through product sales.

Location & Logistics

Address: 557 Durham Rd Unit A, Newtown, PA 18940

Area context: Newtown is in Bucks County, approximately 35 miles northeast of Philadelphia. The area has a growing wellness and natural products consumer base. Del Val's Doylestown campus is roughly 10 miles northwest.

Regulatory context: As a Farm Bill-compliant retailer, Herbal Healers operates under federal and state hemp regulations rather than the medical marijuana framework. This is a lower-cost, lower-complexity regulatory environment that makes it an ideal starting point for a vertically integrated pilot.

How the Partnership Could Work

  • Supply agreement: Greenhouse operation provides consistent, lab-tested flower and clones directly to Herbal Healers
  • Co-branded education: In-store educational materials developed with Del Val, covering cannabinoid science, compliance, and product use
  • Student exposure: Students get retail experience and customer education practice through rotations or internships
  • Social impact visibility: Product lines or labeling that highlight the social impact employment mission
