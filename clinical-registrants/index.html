Clinical Registrants

Pennsylvania's 10 Clinical Registrants

Clinical Registrants (CRs) are the highest-tier permit holders in PA's Medical Marijuana Program. Each CR holds both a grower/processor permit and a dispensary permit, and is partnered with an Academic Clinical Research Center (ACRC) — a medical school at a Pennsylvania university — to conduct cannabis research. Current Status (Mid-2026)

The CR cap of 10 has been fully filled through previous application phases. No new CR applications are being accepted. Changes in CR status occur through acquisitions, permit transfers, or revocations. The DOH only opens new application windows when slots become available.

Working With a CR

For those exploring partnership (e.g., satellite grow site, research collaboration, or work-study programs), here is the realistic path:

  • CR permits are tied to the specific legal entity and its ACRC contract — subcontracting is heavily regulated
  • Any additional site or activity requires DOH review and approval
  • The most viable approach is direct outreach to a CR with a specific proposal that complements their existing research
  • Emphasize education/greenhouse training, social impact employment, and potential research contributions
