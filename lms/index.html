⚙ ISO 9001 Crosswalk — How Modules Meet QMS Requirements

Below is a representative mapping. Every core and operations module contributes to ISO 9001:2015 compliance across multiple clauses. Using these modules as your training backbone gets you close to certification standards — even if you never formally certify.

ISO 9001:2015 ClauseRequirementModule Coverage
7.2 CompetenceDetermine necessary competence of persons doing work; ensure competence based on education, training, or experienceAll 11 modules define specific competence dimensions with 5-level scales. Compliance, Safety, Ethics cover regulatory competence. Dispensary, Budtending, Botany cover operational competence. Analytics, Processing cover technical competence.
7.3 AwarenessEnsure persons are aware of quality policy, relevant QMS objectives, their contribution to QMS effectiveness, and implications of nonconformityCompliance module covers regulatory consequence awareness. Ethics module covers conduct and reporting. Safety module covers hazard awareness and incident reporting.
7.5 Documented InformationMaintain and retain documented information to support QMS operation and demonstrate conformityWorker skill profiles serve as documented training records. Module completion self-reports provide auditable evidence of competence assessment.
8.2.3 Review of Requirements for Products and ServicesOrganization shall ensure it has the ability to meet requirements for products and services to be offeredDispensary module covers patient needs assessment. Supply Chain module covers procurement and supplier qualification. Processing covers QA/QC and batch release.
8.5.1 Control of Production and Service ProvisionControlled conditions including documented procedures, monitoring, and measurementBotany and Advanced Cultivation cover environmental monitoring and SOPs. Processing covers GMP and batch tracking. Analytics covers lab methods and COA compliance.
9.1 Monitoring, Measurement, Analysis and EvaluationDetermine what needs to be monitored and measured, methods, and when results should be analyzedAnalytics module covers HPLC/GC-MS, potency testing, microbial screening. Inventory control in Supply Chain covers reconciliation and discrepancy evaluation.
10.2 Nonconformity and Corrective ActionReact to nonconformity, evaluate need for action, implement action, retain documented informationRegulatory module covers audit preparation, finding response, corrective action plans. Compliance covers incident reporting and documentation.
10.3 Continual ImprovementContinually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the QMSAll modules include skill level advancement paths. Business and Advanced tracks cover process optimization, QbD methodology, and scaling operations.

4

Add Custom SOPs

Layer your own facility-specific SOP modules alongside the standardized curriculum. Workers get one unified training experience.
