Clinical Registrant #2

Agronomed Biologics

Chester, PA-based CR partnered with Drexel University College of Medicine's Medical Cannabis Research Center for evidence-based research on medical cannabis effects.

Overview

Agronomed Biologics holds Clinical Registrant #2 status with a focus on biologics and cannabinoid-based therapeutic research. Based in Chester, PA, the organization brings a pharmaceutical-oriented approach to cannabis medicine, emphasizing rigorous clinical trial methodology and evidence-based treatment protocols.

ACRC Partnership

Drexel University College of Medicine — Philadelphia

Agronomed partners with Drexel's Medical Cannabis Research Center (MCRC), one of Pennsylvania's most active academic cannabis research units. Drexel's College of Medicine brings deep expertise in pharmacology, clinical trial design, and translational research. The MCRC conducts studies on specific medical conditions to determine how cannabis-based treatments can be effectively integrated into patient care protocols.

Dispensary Locations

Agronomed Biologics operates dispensaries under the Liberty Cannabis brand, with six locations across Pennsylvania. All are medical-only.

Research Focus

  • Evidence-based research: Rigorous clinical trials on medical cannabis effects for specific medical conditions
  • Pharmacology infrastructure: Drexel's established pharmacology programs support drug-development-style research protocols
  • Clinical trial capacity: Leveraging Drexel's experience in conducting FDA-standard clinical investigations
  • Cannabinoid therapeutics: Investigating specific cannabinoid profiles for targeted therapeutic applications

Partnership Potential

Agronomed Biologics and Drexel's MCRC offer a strong clinical research environment for partners interested in rigorous trial design and pharmacological investigation. The MCRC's existing infrastructure could support collaborative studies on cultivation-to-clinic product consistency, and student research placements in both lab and clinical settings.
