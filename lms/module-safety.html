Workplace Safety & Security

Covers OSHA compliance, security protocols, cash handling, surveillance systems, and emergency preparedness for cannabis facilities. Required knowledge for all on-site roles.

● Core Requirement ⚙ All On-Site Roles ⏱ ~1.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • OSHA general industry standards for cannabis facilities
  • Security protocols: access control, surveillance, alarm systems
  • Cash management and transport procedures (280E implications)
  • Fire safety, chemical handling, and PPE requirements
  • Emergency action plans and incident reporting

📚 For Learners

Safety knowledge is non-negotiable for any on-site role. This module covers OSHA basics that apply to every cannabis facility — from grow rooms to dispensary floors. A simple differentiator that shows employers you take workplace safety seriously. Earns the Core Trainee badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: First month of any cannabis or horticulture program. Prerequisites: None. Learning objectives: 1) Identify OSHA general industry requirements for cannabis facilities, 2) Describe proper cash handling procedures, 3) Develop an emergency action plan. Discussion prompt: How does the cash-intensive nature of cannabis (IRC 280E) create unique security challenges compared to other retail environments?

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 7.2 Competence, 7.3 Awareness. This module establishes minimum safety competence across your entire workforce. Use it as part of your OSHA compliance program and as documented evidence of safety training. Workers who complete it and self-assess at Level 2+ demonstrate awareness of your safety expectations.
