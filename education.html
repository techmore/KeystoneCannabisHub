Education & Career Pathways

Cannabis Education & Training in Pennsylvania

From certificate programs to hands-on work-study and commercial experience — Pennsylvania offers multiple pathways to build skills and careers in the cannabis and hemp industry.

The Education Gap & Opportunity

Pennsylvania's cannabis industry is growing, but there is a significant gap between academic instruction and real-world commercial experience. Many programs offer theory; few offer structured, hands-on greenhouse and production training under actual compliance and operational conditions.

The proposed Greenhouse Work-Study Augmentation Program is designed to fill this gap — giving students paid or credited experience in clone production, breeding support, cultivation, and processing while supporting a vertically integrated operation.

Available Education Pathways

B.S. Degree

Horticulture w/ Cannabis Specialization

Institution: Delaware Valley University

Full four-year degree combining plant science foundations with specialized cannabis coursework. Includes greenhouse and lab work integrated throughout the program.

Certificate

Cannabis Certificate (17 credits)

Institution: Delaware Valley University

Stand-alone ~1 year program, available full- or part-time. Covers cannabis science, cultivation, processing, and regulatory compliance.

Non-Credit

Green Flower Certificates

Institution: Del Val (in partnership with Green Flower)

Three 9-week online certificates: Cultivation Specialist, Retail Specialist, Extraction & Product Development. $799 each.

Proposed

Greenhouse Work-Study Program

Partner: Del Val + Commercial Operation

Hands-on paid/credited training in clone production, breeding, cultivation, and processing within a compliant hemp operation. Direct pathway to industry employment.

Proposed

Clone Production & Breeding Apprenticeship

Partner: Del Val + Herbal Healers Network

Focused training on genetic selection, tissue culture, clone propagation, and breeding for specific cannabinoid/terpene profiles. Includes change-log research documentation.

Research

ACRC Clinical Research Training

Partner: CRs + University Medical Schools

For advanced students: exposure to clinical research protocols through ACRC partnerships. Limited slots, competitive entry.

Core Skill Areas

The following competencies are developed through the combined education + work-study model:

  • Cultivation: Controlled environment agriculture, lighting, irrigation, nutrient management, IPM
  • Clone Production: Propagation, tissue culture, rooting, transplanting, mother plant maintenance
  • Breeding: Genetic selection, phenotyping, cross-pollination, seed production, change-log documentation
  • Plant Pathology: Disease identification, fungicide management, pathogen population genetics
  • Processing: Harvest, drying, curing, extraction (hemp-derived), product formulation
  • Compliance: Seed-to-sale tracking, lab testing interpretation, regulatory documentation
  • Business: Supply chain management, quality control, standard operating procedures

The Work-Study Model (Proposed)

Students would rotate through operational roles in a compliant greenhouse facility, earning academic credit and/or wages while contributing to real production goals. The curriculum integrates with Del Val's existing certificate and degree programs.

Key Design Features

  • Paid positions (funding via grants, tuition sharing, or production revenue)
  • Structured learning objectives aligned with Del Val competencies
  • Mentorship by experienced growers and researchers
  • Change-log research documentation for breeding data
  • Exposure to compliance, security, and business operations
  • Potential for continued employment after program completion
