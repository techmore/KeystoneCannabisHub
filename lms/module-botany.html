LMS Module · Cultivation

Cannabis Botany & Cultivation

Learn the fundamentals of cannabis plant biology, from propagation through harvest. Covers photoperiods, genetics, pheno hunting, tissue culture, and integrated pest management.

● Cultivation Track ⚙ Growers & Trimmers ⏱ ~3 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Cannabis taxonomy and chemotypes (THC, CBD, CBG, terpene profiles)
  • Photoperiodism: vegetative vs. flowering light cycles
  • Propagation methods: seed, clone, and tissue culture
  • Nutrient management (macronutrients, micronutrients, pH, EC)
  • Pheno hunting: selecting and stabilizing desirable traits
