Dispensary Operations & Patient Care

Master the day-to-day operations of a Pennsylvania medical marijuana dispensary. From patient intake and consultation to inventory management and compliance documentation.

● Dispensary Track ⚙ Agents & Managers ⏱ ~2.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • PA dispensary agent requirements and scope of practice
  • Patient intake workflow: ID verification, certification check, MMJ registry
  • Product forms: flower, vapes, tinctures, topicals, capsules, RSO
  • THC:CBD ratio consultation and dose guidance
  • Inventory reconciliation and METRC daily entry

📚 For Learners

Dispensary agent is the most common entry point in PA cannabis. This module covers exactly what you'll do on day one — patient intake, product recommendations, inventory. Add Budtending for a complete dispensary skill set. Earns the Operations Specialist badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Retail or patient care modules in cannabis business programs. Prerequisites: Compliance module recommended. Learning objectives: 1) Execute patient intake workflow, 2) Match product forms to patient needs, 3) Perform daily inventory reconciliation. Assignment: Write a sample patient consultation script for a first-time medical cannabis user.

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 8.2.3 Review of Requirements, 8.5.1 Control of Production. Use this module as your dispensary agent onboarding standard. New hires who complete it arrive with consistent product knowledge and intake procedures. Reduces per-hire training time and ensures every agent meets minimum competence thresholds.
