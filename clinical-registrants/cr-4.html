Clinical Registrant #4

Laurel Harvest Labs (Cresco)

Eastern PA CR acquired by Cresco Labs in 2021, partnered with Temple University's Lewis Katz School of Medicine and its Center for Substance Abuse Research.

Overview

Laurel Harvest Labs was awarded Clinical Registrant #4 status and operated as an independent CR until its acquisition by Cresco Labs in 2021. Cresco is one of the largest multi-state cannabis operators in the United States, and the acquisition brought significant capital and operational scale to the CR permit. Temple University's Lewis Katz School of Medicine continues as the ACRC partner.

ACRC Partnership

Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University — Philadelphia

Temple's Center for Substance Abuse Research (CSAR) leads the ACRC collaboration with Cresco. CSAR has a strong track record of research on pain pathways, addiction mechanisms, and the therapeutic potential of cannabinoids. Temple brings expertise in neuropharmacology, behavioral pharmacology, and clinical research design to the partnership.

Dispensary Locations

Originally Laurel Harvest Labs, now operating under Cresco Labs' Sunnyside brand. Cresco has the largest retail footprint in PA with 11+ dispensaries statewide. All are medical-only.

Research Focus

  • COPD: Investigating medical cannabis effects on chronic obstructive pulmonary disease symptoms and management
  • Post-incisional pain: Studying cannabinoid efficacy for post-surgical pain management
  • Eosinophilic esophagitis (EOE): Exploring cannabis-based treatments for this inflammatory condition
  • Sleep disorders from chronic pain: Researching cannabis effects on sleep quality in chronic pain patients
  • Neuropathic pain: Clinical studies on cannabinoid treatment for nerve-based pain conditions

Partnership Potential

The Cresco-Temple partnership offers significant scale and research infrastructure. Temple's CSAR provides a strong scientific foundation for collaborations on pain research, addiction studies, and neuropharmacology. Potential partners could engage through student research placements, clinical trial support, or cultivation-to-clinic product development studies.
