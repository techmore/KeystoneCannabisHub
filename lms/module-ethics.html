Ethics & Professionalism

Develop the professional standards and ethical frameworks needed to serve patients, colleagues, and the community with integrity in Pennsylvania's cannabis industry.

● Core Requirement ⚙ All Roles ⏱ ~1.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Professional codes of conduct for cannabis workers
  • Patient privacy, dignity, and informed consent
  • Diversity, equity, and inclusion in the cannabis workplace
  • Conflict resolution and professional communication
  • Reporting misconduct and whistleblower protections

📚 For Learners

Ethics and professionalism is what separates a great cannabis employee from a good one. This module helps you communicate better, handle conflicts professionally, and understand DEI in the cannabis workplace. These skills transfer to any role in any industry. Earns the Core Trainee badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Alongside professional development or capstone courses. Prerequisites: None. Learning objectives: 1) Apply ethical frameworks to cannabis workplace scenarios, 2) Demonstrate culturally competent patient communication, 3) Identify whistleblower protections. Discussion prompt: How does the legacy of cannabis prohibition and disproportionate enforcement create unique DEI challenges for the legal industry?

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 7.2 Competence, 7.3 Awareness, 10.2 Nonconformity. Use this module to establish a baseline standard for professional conduct across your organization. Demonstrates commitment to ethical operations and DEI — increasingly important in DOH and investor evaluations.
