Supply Chain & Logistics

Learn the end-to-end cannabis supply chain: from cultivation to processing to dispensary. Covers transportation regulations, warehousing, inventory management, and METRC tracking.

● Operations Track ⚙ Ops & Logistics Staff ⏱ ~2 hours

What You'll Learn

  • PA cannabis transportation rules and chain of custody
  • Warehousing and storage requirements (environmental, security)
  • Inventory reconciliation and lot tracking in METRC
  • Procurement strategies and supplier qualification
  • Cross-docking, distribution models, and route optimization

📚 For Learners

Supply chain and logistics is a growing career path in cannabis independent of cultivation or retail knowledge. This module prepares you for inventory, transportation, and warehousing roles — skills that transfer to any regulated industry. Earns the Operations Specialist badge.

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Supply chain management or operations courses. Prerequisites: Compliance module recommended. Learning objectives: 1) Describe PA cannabis transportation rules, 2) Explain METRC inventory tracking, 3) Develop a procurement strategy. Discussion prompt: How does METRC's seed-to-sale tracking requirement create both compliance burdens and operational data opportunities?

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 8.5.1 Control of Production, 9.1 Monitoring. This module standardizes supply chain knowledge across your logistics team. Ensures consistent understanding of chain of custody, inventory control, and transportation compliance. Essential for audit readiness and DOH inspection preparation.
