Cannabis Analytics & Lab Testing

Understand the analytical methods used to test cannabis products for potency, purity, and safety. Covers HPLC, GC-MS, microbial screening, mycotoxin testing, and Certificate of Analysis (COA) interpretation.

● Processing Track ⚙ Lab Techs & QC Staff ⏱ ~2.5 hours

What You'll Learn

  • HPLC and GC-MS principles for cannabinoid and terpene analysis
  • Microbiological testing requirements in PA: bacteria, mold, yeast, mycotoxins
  • Residual solvent and pesticide screening
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) structure and critical values
  • PA DOH testing requirements and compliance thresholds
