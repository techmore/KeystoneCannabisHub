Clinical Registrant #5

CannTech PA / Ayr Wellness

Western Pennsylvania CR serving the Erie region, partnered with Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine for osteopathic approaches to cannabis therapeutics.

Overview

CannTech PA LLC holds Clinical Registrant #5 status and operates under the Ayr Wellness brand, one of the largest multi-state cannabis operators in the country. Serving the Western Pennsylvania and Erie region, CannTech PA brings Ayr's national operational expertise to Pennsylvania's medical marijuana program while maintaining a strong focus on osteopathic medical research through its LECOM partnership.

ACRC Partnership

Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine (LECOM) — Erie

LECOM is one of the nation's largest medical schools and a leader in osteopathic medical education. The partnership with CannTech PA focuses on osteopathic approaches to cannabis therapeutics, emphasizing the whole-patient philosophy that distinguishes osteopathic medicine. LECOM's Erie campus provides a unique regional perspective on cannabis medicine in northwestern Pennsylvania.

Research Focus

  • Osteopathic approaches: Integrating cannabis therapeutics with osteopathic manipulative treatment and holistic patient care
  • Pain management: Researching cannabis efficacy for chronic pain through an osteopathic lens
  • Medical education: Developing cannabis curriculum for osteopathic medical students
  • Rural health: Studying cannabis access and outcomes in Pennsylvania's rural and underserved communities

Partnership Potential

The CannTech PA-LECOM partnership offers unique opportunities in osteopathic medical education and rural health research. Potential collaborations include student research rotations, clinical education program development, and studies on cannabis access in rural communities. Ayr's national infrastructure also provides scalability for larger research initiatives.
