Advanced Cultivation & Facility Management

Take your cultivation knowledge to the next level. Covers commercial-scale facility design, HVAC, lighting systems, pheno hunting programs, SOP development, and production planning.

● Advanced Track ⚙ Master Growers & Facility Mgrs ⏱ ~3 hours

What You'll Learn

  • Commercial facility design: flower rooms, veg rooms, mother rooms, drying/curing
  • HVAC, dehumidification, CO2 enrichment, and environmental control systems
  • Lighting systems: HPS, CMH, LED spectrum selection and PPFD mapping
  • Pheno hunting program design and genetic stabilization
  • Production scheduling, yield forecasting, and SOP management

📚 For Learners

Ready to move beyond entry-level cultivation? This module covers the facility design, environmental control, and production planning knowledge needed for master grower and facility manager roles. Completing Botany + Advanced Cultivation signals you're serious about a career in cannabis horticulture. Earns the Master Grower badge (at Level 4+).

🎓 For Educators

Suggested placement: Upper-level horticulture or facility management courses. Prerequisites: Botany module or equivalent. Learning objectives: 1) Design a commercial cannabis facility layout, 2) Calculate HVAC and lighting loads, 3) Develop a pheno hunting program. Discussion prompt: How do facility design decisions (lighting spectrum, HVAC capacity, room configuration) affect production economics at scale?

🏢 For Employers

ISO 9001 mapping: 8.5.1 Control of Production, 10.3 Continual Improvement. Use this module to upskill cultivation leadership and standardize facility management knowledge. Ensures your grow team operates from a shared understanding of environmental control, production planning, and quality optimization.
