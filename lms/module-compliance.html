LMS Module · Core Compliance

PA Cannabis Law & Compliance

Understand the legal framework governing Pennsylvania's medical marijuana program, patient privacy requirements, seed-to-sale tracking, and compliance fundamentals. Required for all roles.

● Core Requirement ⚙ All Roles ⏱ ~2 hours

What You'll Learn

  • PA Medical Marijuana Act — history, structure, key provisions
  • HIPAA Privacy & Security Rules for patient information
  • Seed-to-sale tracking and METRC compliance
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) reading and interpretation
  • DOH inspection protocols and documentation requirements
