Proposed Platform

PA Cannabis Job Placement

A blind talent matching platform that connects university training, skills assessment, and company success metrics to make unbiased job recommendations across Pennsylvania's cannabis industry.

What This Is

This is a concept and blueprint for a job placement platform that sits alongside the LMS Portal. Where the LMS certifies that someone has completed training, this platform validates that they can actually do the work — and matches them to employers who need exactly what they offer, without the bias that traditionally filters out good candidates. It is not built yet.

The Problem

Right now, getting hired in PA cannabis comes down to who you know, where you went to school, and whether your resume happens to match a job description. Employers waste time interviewing candidates who look good on paper but can't perform. Good workers get passed over because they lack the right brand name on their resume or the right connection. Everyone loses.

  • Universities produce graduates but can't prove they're job-ready beyond a transcript
  • Workers have training certificates but no way to demonstrate real skill level
  • Employers spend weeks interviewing to find what a skills test would reveal in an hour
  • The industry perpetuates hiring bias — name, school, gender, and network matter more than ability

The Solution: Blind Matching in 4 Steps

1

Understand the Training

We ingest curriculum data from every PA university cannabis program (Jefferson, Wilkes, York, PIT, Del Val, Central Penn, HACC, etc.) and map it to industry-standard competencies. When a graduate from any program enters the platform, we already know what they've studied — not by degree title, but by actual course content.

See full university directory for program details.

2

Test the Individual

Candidates take role-specific skills assessments built by industry practitioners. Tests measure real competency: compliance scenarios, product knowledge, customer interactions, inventory math, system operations. Results are scored against anonymized benchmarks — not against a curve. Testing is blind to name, school, age, gender, and background.

Assessments are aligned with the LMS Portal's training modules. A candidate who completes the LMS Compliance track plus passes the skills assessment holds a verified credential that means something to any employer.

3

Measure Company Success Criteria

Employers define what success looks like for each role — not as a wishlist, but as measurable criteria: proficiency level in specific systems, communication style preferences, experience with certain product types, shift availability, autonomy level. The platform learns from placement outcomes — which criteria actually predict retention and performance — and refines matches over time.

4

Blind Recommendation

Employers see candidates with all identifying information removed — no name, no school name, no age, no gender, no ethnicity, no photo. Only what matters: skill scores, competency certifications, experience level, availability, and a blind compatibility score against the company's success criteria. The employer shortlists based on fit alone. Unblinding happens only at the interview stage.

Role-Specific Tracks

Every track aligns with both the LMS training modules and the skills assessment. A candidate progresses through: LMS training → skills assessment → blind placement. Each track includes specific competencies tested, certifications earned, and the types of roles it maps to.

🏡 Front-End Sales & Patient Care

For dispensary agents, patient consultants, and retail leads. The public face of any cannabis business.

  • Patient consultation & needs assessment
  • Product knowledge across all categories (flower, vapes, concentrates, edibles, topicals, tinctures)
  • POS system operation & transaction compliance
  • Customer service scenarios & de-escalation
  • HIPAA compliance & patient privacy in practice
  • Recommendation-driven selling (not just order-taking)

📦 Back-End Fulfillment & Inventory

For inventory specialists, fulfillment leads, and operations coordinators. The engine room of every dispensary and processor.

  • Inventory receiving, verification & reconciliation
  • Seed-to-sale system data entry & compliance
  • Waste documentation & destruction protocols
  • Product staging, rotation & expiration management
  • Physical inventory counts & discrepancy investigation
  • Supply chain coordination with cultivation & processing

🌱 Cultivation & Production

For grow technicians, cultivation leads, and facility managers. The people who produce the product.

  • Plant life cycle management (clone to harvest)
  • Nutrient management & irrigation systems
  • Integrated pest management (IPM) protocols
  • Environmental monitoring & HVAC control
  • Harvest, trim, cure & packaging
  • Data collection for breeding & phenotype tracking

⚙ Processing & Extraction

For extraction technicians, processing leads, and lab assistants. The people who transform plant into product.

  • Extraction equipment operation (CO2, ethanol, hydrocarbon)
  • Solvent safety, handling & storage protocols
  • Post-processing: winterization, distillation, crystallization
  • Quality control sampling & in-process testing
  • GMP sanitation & contamination prevention
  • Batch tracking & lot reconciliation

📊 Accounting, Finance & Administration

For accountants, bookkeepers, and admin staff. The backbone that keeps cannabis businesses compliant with tax law and payroll.

  • IRC 280E implications for cannabis businesses
  • Inventory valuation & cost of goods sold (COGS) tracking
  • Cash management, deposit procedures & reconciliation
  • Payroll processing with cannabis-specific considerations
  • Sales tax & excise tax reporting
  • Compliance reporting for DOH & PDA audits

🖥 Technology, Systems & Data

For system administrators, data analysts, and tech support. The people who keep the digital infrastructure running.

  • METRC / seed-to-sale platform administration
  • POS system configuration & troubleshooting
  • Inventory management software operation
  • Data analysis & compliance reporting
  • Security system monitoring & access control
  • Integration between platforms (POS + METRC + accounting)

📈 Management & Leadership

For department heads, store managers, and aspiring leaders. The people who build and run teams.

  • Team management, scheduling & performance reviews
  • Regulatory compliance program management
  • Financial planning, P&L analysis & budgeting
  • Cannabis-specific HR, hiring & employee policies
  • Strategic planning & operations scaling
  • Social equity program design & inclusive hiring

🌎 Compliance & Regulatory

For compliance officers, auditors, and quality assurance staff. The people who keep the license.

  • DOH inspection preparation & response
  • Recordkeeping & documentation management
  • Standard operating procedure (SOP) writing & maintenance
  • Product recall & adverse event reporting protocols
  • Employee training program management
  • Third-party lab audit & COA verification

How the Matching Works

For candidates:

  1. Complete LMS training modules for your target track
  2. Take the skills assessment (online, proctored, ~1–2 hours)
  3. Receive a competency profile with scores across each skill dimension
  4. Set your preferences: location, schedule, company size, culture factors
  5. Get notified when an employer is looking for your profile
  6. Employer sees your blind profile — if they shortlist, you're unblinded and connected

For employers:

  1. Define the role: required skill levels, preferred experience, cultural indicators, schedule needs
  2. Set success criteria — the system learns what predicts good hires for your specific operation
  3. Review blind candidate profiles — names and schools hidden, only scores and fit data shown
  4. Shortlist candidates for interview (unblinding happens here)
  5. After hire, provide outcome data: did the candidate perform as predicted?
  6. The system improves its matching algorithm based on real outcomes

For universities:

  1. Integrate curriculum data so the platform understands what your program covers
  2. Receive anonymized reports on graduate performance across skill dimensions
  3. Identify gaps between your curriculum and industry needs
  4. Adjust program content based on real employer feedback
  5. Track placement rates and starting positions of your graduates

The Full Ecosystem

These two platforms work together — the LMS Portal provides the standardized training backbone, and the Job Placement Platform validates and matches. Together they form a complete talent infrastructure for PA cannabis.
